Privacy Policy

RemindMe

RemindMe is a utility app for reminders, alarms, appointments, notes, goals, counters and medication schedules. I publish and operate RemindMe. This policy explains the processing performed by the Android app.

1. Controller and contact

I am the controller for the personal data processed by RemindMe. For privacy questions or rights requests, contact me at bilal.elgin.info@gmail.com.

2. Data processed

RemindMe can store the reminders, appointments, dates, times, places, descriptions, notes, medication details, alarm settings, counters, attachments and other information that you choose to enter or save. Text, images, PDF pages and voice-recognition results are processed only when you request the related feature. Location is optional and is used for alarm weather; the last authorized coordinates and time may be stored locally. The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect advertising identifiers, analytics profiles or contact lists.

3. Local processing and intelligent understanding

The deterministic semantic engine analyzes text on the phone and extracts possible dates, times, addresses, people, organizations, reminders and form fields. OCR uses PaddleOCR-VL locally after I obtain your confirmation to download its model. The OCR model and the optional local language model are verified before installation. No reminder text, saved note, OCR document or local semantic-analysis input is sent to an AI server. Results are suggestions: you review and save them yourself.

4. Voice and external speech services

Voice dictation uses the Android speech-recognition service selected on your device. That service may process audio on the device or send it to its provider, and may require an internet connection. The provider controls that processing under its own terms. RemindMe does not create a permanent audio recording; the recognized text is shown for your review.

5. Network services

When you use the relevant feature, the app may contact Hugging Face to download a consented model, GitHub to check optional semantic-knowledge updates, Pastebin to check optional update metadata, and Open-Meteo to retrieve weather for an authorized location. These services receive ordinary connection or request data and, where applicable, the requested coordinates or public update/model request; they do not receive your saved reminders or OCR documents from RemindMe. External links, sharing and installation of an update are user initiated.

6. Permissions

Camera is requested only for a photo. Microphone is requested only for dictation. Location is requested only for optional alarm weather. Notifications, exact alarms, vibration, full-screen alarm access, boot reception and foreground-service permissions support reminders, alarms and user-started downloads or analyses. Internet is used for the network services described above. You may refuse optional permissions; the related feature may then be unavailable while ordinary reminders continue.

7. Storage and retention

Saved data remains in the app’s private storage until you delete it, remove the item or uninstall the app. Temporary camera files are deleted after processing or cancellation. A canceled model download may retain an uninstalled partial model so a later user-approved attempt can resume; it is not treated as usable until verification succeeds. Android backups are disabled in the manifest.

8. Legal bases and sharing

I process data entered by you to provide the requested app functions, based on performance of the requested service and, where applicable, your consent for permissions or optional downloads. I use legitimate interests to maintain security and optional update mechanisms. I do not sell personal data or share saved reminder content for advertising. A third-party speech provider, weather service or download host may process network metadata, and the speech provider may process audio as described above.

9. Rights and complaints

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or information about processing by emailing me. You may withdraw consent for an optional permission or feature at any time; this does not affect earlier lawful processing. You may complain to the supervisory authority, including the CNIL in France. I will respond within the period required by applicable law.

10. Children and changes

RemindMe is a general-purpose reminder utility and has no age restriction specific to its functionality. I do not knowingly design it to collect children’s personal data. I may update this policy when the app’s processing changes and will publish the current version at the policy address.
