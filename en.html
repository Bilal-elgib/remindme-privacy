Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

This policy explains how RemindMe handles information when you use the Android app. It is based on the current app behavior.

Publisher and contact

The app’s About screen identifies Bilal ELGIN as its developer. For privacy questions, contact bilal.elgin.info@gmail.com.

Account and data sent to a server

RemindMe does not offer account registration, cloud synchronization, or an app-operated server. This build does not request the INTERNET permission and does not automatically transmit saved app data. If you open an external Telegram link or use Android sharing, the action is initiated by you and is handled by the selected receiving app.

Data processed in the app

Information the user chooses to save may include titles, descriptions, locations, dates, times, repeat settings, note text, medication names and details, counter settings, sound options, language, theme, and notification preferences. Free-text fields and attached files may contain personal information that the user decides to enter. Some medication-related information may be sensitive or health-related.

For attachments, the user selects images or PDFs using the Android picker. RemindMe copies the selected content to the app's private internal storage. The code limits each file to 100 MB. Attachments are used to show a preview, open the document, or associate it with the relevant item.

Storage and Android backup

Appointments and other records are stored in a Room database and private app preferences; attachments are stored in the app’s internal directory. The manifest sets android:allowBackup="false", disabling Android Auto Backup for this app. Device-to-device transfer behavior can nevertheless vary by Android version and device manufacturer. The source does not indicate additional app-level encryption, and RemindMe has no built-in backup or restore function.

Notifications and permissions

RemindMe schedules notifications and alarms for features selected by the user. Android 13 and later may request notification permission. From Android 12, exact-alarm access is a special optional setting; without it, Android may deliver alerts later. On Android 14 and later, full-screen alarm access is separately controlled, and a standard notification remains available if it is denied. Camera permission is requested only when the user enables the flash option; it controls the torch and is not used to capture photos or video. Vibration, reboot restoration, wake locks, and foreground media playback support reminders. Android and device-maker restrictions may delay or limit delivery.

Sharing with other apps

The user can choose to share an appointment and, if desired, its attachments, or to open an attachment in another app. Android then displays compatible apps, and the transfer occurs only after the user's action. Shared text may contain the appointment title, date, time, location, and description. If the user chooses to include files, the relevant images or PDFs are sent to the receiving app with read access.

After this transfer, data processing is handled by the app and provider chosen by the user. Consult their respective policies. RemindMe does not control the data received or its retention by these third parties.

Retention and deletion

Items remain in the app until the user deletes them, clears app data, or uninstalls the app. RemindMe does not keep a server-side copy. Because Android Auto Backup is disabled in this build, app data is not included in that backup mechanism; device-to-device transfer can vary by manufacturer.

Security

Work data is stored in the private locations assigned to the app by Android. The code audit did not identify separate app-level encryption. Android Auto Backup is disabled in this build. Device security, screen lock, and any app selected for user-initiated sharing remain under the user’s control and the relevant providers’ policies.

Children and audience

The publisher must declare the target audience and minimum age in Google Play according to the intended use and content of the app. This policy does not presume that the app is, or is not, intended for children. If the audience or features change, this policy and the Play declarations must be updated.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact bilal.elgin.info@gmail.com.
