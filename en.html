    • 7. Notifications, alarms, and permissions

    RemindMe uses the Android capabilities needed to deliver features you enable. Depending on your Android version and choices, these include:

    • Notifications: notification permission allows reminders and other alerts to be displayed. You can deny or change it in Android settings.
    • Exact alarms and full-screen alerts: optional Android-controlled access can help alarms run close to the selected time or appear prominently. If access is denied, Android may deliver an alert later or use a standard notification instead.
    • Vibration, wake lock, and foreground services: these support alert vibration, reminder delivery, alarm audio playback, and visible foreground downloads. Battery and device-maker policies can still delay or limit background behavior.
    • Boot completion: after a device restart, the app can restore the scheduling information needed for reminders.
    • Camera and microphone: these are requested only for the user-selected camera, flashlight, and voice features described above.
    • Location: approximate or precise location is used only for the optional alarm-weather feature described above.

    Android may display permission prompts and control availability differently across versions and devices. RemindMe does not treat permission grants as a guarantee that Android will deliver every notification at the exact requested time.

    8. Sharing and opening files

    You may share an appointment and optionally its attachments, or open an attachment in another application. Shared text can include the title, date, time, location, and description. If you include files, the selected images or PDFs are provided to the receiving application with the access Android grants for that action. Processing after the transfer is controlled by the receiving application or service. Review its privacy policy before sharing.

    9. Retention and deletion

    RemindMe keeps saved items locally until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. You can delete saved items using the deletion options available in the app; deleting an item removes its record and reminders. Cleanup of associated attachment files depends on the item type and may be deferred, so RemindMe does not promise that every associated file is removed immediately in every deletion flow. Temporary camera captures and temporary OCR page images are deleted after processing finishes or is cancelled.

    Clearing app data or uninstalling RemindMe can permanently remove locally stored items. Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app, and RemindMe has no built-in backup or restore feature. Device-to-device transfer behavior may nevertheless vary by Android version and manufacturer. Network providers and third-party services may retain their own connection logs or data independently of RemindMe; deletion from RemindMe does not delete information already sent to those providers or shared with another app.

    10. Security

    RemindMe stores its working data in Android’s private app storage and does not identify separate app-level encryption in the supplied implementation. Model downloads use HTTPS and checksum verification. No security measure can eliminate every risk. Protect your device, screen lock, Android account, and any third-party application with access to information you choose to share.

    11. Legal bases and GDPR rights

    Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, RemindMe processes local app data to provide the features you request, based on performance of the service you choose to use. Permission-based features, such as location, camera, microphone, and notifications, depend on your choice and can generally be disabled in Android settings. Optional model downloads and optional weather use also depend on your choice. Where a legitimate interest or another lawful basis applies to limited technical or security processing, the applicable basis is assessed in context.

    Subject to the GDPR and its conditions, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, portability of data you provided, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. You may also lodge a complaint with the data-protection supervisory authority in your country or place of residence.

    Because RemindMe is primarily local and has no app-operated account server, you can usually exercise access, correction, and deletion directly by reviewing or editing items in the app, deleting items, clearing app data, disabling permissions, or uninstalling the app. These local actions do not control copies held by Android speech providers, Open-Meteo, Hugging Face, GitHub, Pastebin, a receiving app, or another website. For a request concerning processing for which you need the developer’s assistance, use the public repository contact route in section 1.

    12. Children

    RemindMe is a general productivity app and is not designed for children. It is not directed specifically at children, and the developer does not knowingly seek personal information from children through an account or profile system.

    13. Changes to this policy

    I may update this policy when RemindMe’s features, permissions, network resources, or data practices change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. Continued use of a changed version means that you have had an opportunity to review the updated policy.
